  • ROBRT: robust algorithms for phylogenetic regression
  • EvoDA: supervised learning of trait evolution
  • pSTD: Probabilistic distances between species tree models. pSTD is used to compute phylogenetic distances under the multispecies coalescent model for species trees and reticulating networks.
  • PRDATR (PRobabilistic Distances under models of Adaptive Trait evolution in R): An R program that computes the probabilistic distances between phylogenetic models of continuous trait evolution.
  • SpeciesTopoTestR Likelihood-based hypothesis tests of species topologies in R
  • PhyloWGA: phylogenetic analyses and interrogation of whole genome alignments. PhyloWHA is user-friendly R package for improving chromosome-scale phylogenetic analyses
  • GppFst Genomic posterior predictive simulations of Fst and Dxy. Gppfst takes as input a joint posterior distribution of population demographic parameters for two populations (effective population sizes, divergence times, etc). GppFst uses this posterior to generate null distributions of population statistics (i.e., Fst, Dxy) that can be used to investigate genomic evidence of selection and other processes.
  • ThetaMater: Bayesian estimation of the population size parameter. ThetaMater employs an infinite-sites likelihood function to estimate the effective population size parameter from genomic data.
  • TraitTrainR: Organized simulator of continuous trait evolution.
  • Amphibian Karyotype Database: Database for amphibian karyotypes.

    • Teaching: Past, Present, and Future

    Applied Regression

    Graduate-level course on regression procedures, models, and applications. Generalized linear models, regularization, model building, feature selection, dimensionality reduction, shrinkage, non-linear methods and basis expansions, Bayesian regression, logistic regression and other classifier algorithms, other facets of supervised machine learning for regression problems.

    Design and Analysis of Experiments

    Graduate-level course on design and analyses of experimental data. Experimental design, linear models, ANOVA, ANCOVA, hypothesis testing, model adequacy and diagnostics, balanced designs, inbalanced designs, factorial designs, confounded designs, blocks, variance components, generalized linear models, linear mixed models, and more.

    Introduction to Data Science for Biologists

    Learn an array of skills for handling “Big Data” in biology and beyond! Data wrangling, visualization, hypothesis testing, inference, predictive modeling, supervised and unsupervised learning introductions

    Principles of Experimention

    Graduate-level course on design of experiments. Variable and data-types, hypothesis testing, grad-level introductory probability and statistics, distributions, categorical data analyses, introduction to regression, experimental designs, F-tests, and other hypotheses tests
